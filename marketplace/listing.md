# Marketplace listing

## Display name

Coruma Health Data

## Short description

Read your authorized Apple Health history through a private, read-only connection.

## Long description

Connect AI assistants to the Apple Health data you explicitly authorize in Coruma. Summarize activity, sleep, workouts, heart rate, energy, and body measurements; check synchronization freshness; and retrieve optional workout GPS routes. Access is read-only, limited by OAuth scopes, and revocable from Coruma. Coruma is a personal data portability tool, not a medical device, and does not diagnose, treat, or recommend care.
